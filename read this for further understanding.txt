Mochi AI DeskMate: Core Functionality
Mochi acts as a versatile AI-driven bridge between your physical desk and your PC. At its heart, it utilizes a real-time AI stream that processes requests to launch YouTube videos, perform targeted web searches, or execute system-level media controls for Windows (Play/Pause, Track Skip, and Volume). You can customize Mochi’s persona instantly by modifying the system prompt or resetting its logic via memory.json. Its physical presence is enhanced by an LDR-based night mode and a "Pastel Spiral" RGB lighting system that adds a calm aesthetic, while a passive buzzer provides tactile audio feedback to make the interface feel alive.
(Note: The RGB and buzzer systems are modular; while currently configured for base feedback, they are pre-wired for future expansion, such as reacting to PC audio, AI mood-state, or custom sound synthesis.)
